### 🧠 Design Intent (Why)
GUI agents are highly vulnerable to indirect prompt injection (pop-ups/ads/UGC). This adds two of the
field-standard layers as defense-in-depth for *ordinary* actions: an always-on instruction defense
(spotlighting in the system prompt) and a cheap detector that flags injection-like screen text to the
model. It is deliberately tight (no false-positive churn on real UI) and a detector only — it never
drops the content the agent needs. Probabilistic, not a guarantee: secrets stay out of the loop (L0)
and irreversible actions still gate (L4, next). Full CaMeL / a model-based classifier remain future
(BACKLOG).

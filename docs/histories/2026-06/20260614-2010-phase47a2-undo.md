### 🧠 Design Intent (Why)
Destructive actions are exactly where an undo-able Snackbar beats a Toast: it confirms what
happened *and* gives a recovery path, which matters most for "clear key" / "delete all" where a
misclick is costly. Undo is implemented by capturing pre-state in memory and re-saving on tap
(no tombstones, no schema change) — cheap and reliable for the snackbar's lifetime. The History
delete logic is centralized in one composable so the phone and two-pane surfaces can't drift.
Kept the PR focused on the destructive subset (the remaining Toasts are benign "busy" notices)
and deferred spacing/type tokens + reduce-motion transition wrappers to 4.7a-3, mirroring how
4.7a was split to stay green and reviewable.

### 🧠 Design Intent (Why)
Closes the transparency layer: after acting, the agent tells you *what it did* in one line, and a
first-run explainer introduces the trust model before it's encountered mid-task. The receipt counts
only successful actions and is built from a tool tally (not fragile transcript parsing); the pure
summarizer is the tested unit. With this, Phase 6.5c (L0–L4) is complete — the remaining work is the
on-device `V143–V159` verification pass on the working debug APK.
